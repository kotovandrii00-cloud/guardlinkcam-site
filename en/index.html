Live video and history

From the big picture
to the right moment.

Availability depends on your camera, server, region, network and account permissions.

Xiaomi

Sign in to the provider account, choose compatible cameras, view native live video and browse microSD recordings. Archive playback is silent; Xiaomi marks identify an entire recording segment, not the precise moment of motion. Paid cloud archive access is not included.

Milestone / Husky

Connect to Mobile Server for live video, a motion interval journal and recorded playback. Audio requires an available microphone and the appropriate server permissions.

Streams and local saves

Compatible RTSP/H.264, HLS and ONVIF sources. Take snapshots and manually record silent video locally, then use the system share menu to export. RTSP audio is not supported.

Live camera audio

Compatible Xiaomi streams support G.711, PCM and Opus. Availability depends on the model and stream. Two-way talk is not claimed.

Data you can understand

Clear connections.
Control over your data.

Credentials and session keys are stored in Keychain. Cameras and provider services receive the information needed for your chosen connection.

The developer does not run a backend that receives or stores your camera video. The app connects to the cameras, servers and provider services you configure. Their data processing follows their own policies. Read about data and deletion →

Ajax alerts work while the app is active. The standard release does not include background push notifications. GuardLinkCam does not replace an alarm system, monitoring service or emergency services.
